Arcana Rift

Spells & abilities

Five spells, each with its own visuals and its own reason to exist in a fight.

Pebbles

Launches a quick barrage of five small rocks. Low damage, but it fires fast.

Fireball

A blazing fireball doing average damage. Classic and reliable.

Slash

An energy slash right in front of you. Low damage, but almost no wind-up.

Laser Beam

A piercing beam that hits everything in a straight line. The highest damage in the game.

Poison Splash

Hurls a blob of poison that splashes into a toxic pool — which damages enemies and you alike.

Every one of these spell sprites was drawn by me, by hand, in pixel art.
