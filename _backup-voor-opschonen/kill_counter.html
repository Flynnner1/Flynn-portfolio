Kill counter

Tracks what you've killed, per type — and remembers it after you close the game.

How it works

  • The counter updates in real time as you defeat slimes, skeletons, zombies and everything else.
  • Press K to bring up your totals broken down per enemy type.
  • It doubles as a personal high score, and as proof of how many rare variants you've actually managed to find.

Saving and loading

The totals survive between sessions through Unity's PlayerPrefs. Every kill writes the new value straight away, and the counts are read back in on startup: 

// when you kill an enemy
killCount++;
PlayerPrefs.SetInt("KillCount", killCount);
PlayerPrefs.Save();

// load the counts when the game starts
void Start() {
    LoadCounts();
}
