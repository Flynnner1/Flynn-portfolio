Levelling system

Levels aren't just a number here — they're the currency you spend on getting stronger.

How it works

  • Gaining XP — you earn experience by defeating enemies, and the tougher and rarer the enemy, the more it gives.
  • XP bar — a dedicated bar on the HUD shows how close you are to the next level.
  • Levelling up — fill the bar and you level. Each new level costs 5 more XP than the last, so progress slows as you climb.
  • Stat progression — every level up raises your core stats, such as max health and movement speed.

Spending levels

On top of the automatic gains, each level you hold can be spent in the shop as currency for a permanent upgrade. The prices are deliberately uneven:

  • +5 max HP — costs 1 level
  • Faster movement speed — costs 5 levels

That gap is the actual decision in the system: bank your levels for one expensive upgrade, or spend them steadily on survivability. See how health factors in on the HP system page.
