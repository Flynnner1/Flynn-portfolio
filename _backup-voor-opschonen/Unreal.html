Mech Target Shooter

A first-person shooter mini-game where every hit powers up a mech animation.

Overview

The goal is to shoot targets as accurately as possible. Every hit advances a mech animation one step further, which turns the score into something you can see instead of just read — and makes each shot feel like it landed. Hit enough targets and you win.

Features

  • First-person target shooting mechanics
  • Hit detection with score and progress tracking
  • A mech animation that advances with each successful hit
  • A win condition once you reach the target count

Technologies used

  • Unity Engine
  • C# for gameplay scripting
  • Unity's animation system for the mech sequence

What I learned

  • Building first-person shooter controls and interactions
  • Making hit detection reliable rather than approximately right
  • Triggering animations from gameplay events
  • Designing a clear loop with a win condition that feels earned
