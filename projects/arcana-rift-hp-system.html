Health system

The core of survival — and the reason levelling up matters.

How it works

  • Health bar — your current health sits permanently on the HUD as a red bar, so you can read your state at a glance.
  • Taking damage — every enemy hit drops your HP.
  • Healing — you restore health by buying it in one of the shops.
  • Defeat — at zero HP you're beaten. You respawn at the last checkpoint, but you lose some XP and coins as a penalty.

Growing stronger

Extra maximum HP is one of the upgrades you can buy through the levelling system, at a cost of 1 level each time. More health is what lets you start taking on the rarer, harder enemies.
