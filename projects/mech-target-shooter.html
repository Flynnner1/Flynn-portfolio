Mech Target Shooter

A first-person shooter mini-game where every hit powers up a mech animation.

The reward: hit enough targets and the mech runs its full animation on the arena floor.

Overview

The goal is to shoot targets as accurately as possible. Every hit advances a mech animation one step further, which turns the score into something you can see instead of just read — and makes each shot feel like it landed. Hit enough targets and you win.

Features

  • Standard first-person controls, including jumping
  • Projectiles that follow physics instead of hitting instantly
  • Targets that break when you hit them, and a score that goes up
  • A mech animation that advances one step with every successful hit
  • A win once every target is destroyed, with a UI to restart
  • Playable with keyboard and mouse or with a controller

And the part before that: working through the targets on the island.

Technologies used

  • Unreal Engine 5
  • Blueprints for the gameplay logic
  • Unreal's animation system for the mech sequence
  • Materials and models, so the level does not look like an empty demo

What I learned

  • Finding my way around a second engine after working mostly in Unity
  • Building gameplay in Blueprints instead of in written code
  • Making hit detection reliable rather than approximately right
  • Triggering animations from gameplay events
  • Designing a clear loop with a win condition that feels earned
